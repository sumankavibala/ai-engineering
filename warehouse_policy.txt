Warehouse Receiving Policy

All incoming shipments must be inspected before they are accepted into inventory.

The receiving employee must verify the purchase order number, supplier name, item quantity, and item condition.

Damaged goods must be separated from accepted inventory and reported to the warehouse supervisor.

If the received quantity differs from the purchase order quantity, the discrepancy must be recorded in the warehouse management system.

Items must not be moved to available inventory until the receiving inspection has been completed.

Warehouse employees must scan the item barcode before moving an item into a storage location.

High-value items require supervisor approval before being placed into inventory.

Inventory adjustments must be approved by an authorized warehouse manager.
